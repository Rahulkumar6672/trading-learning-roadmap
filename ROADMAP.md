### By holding period

- Intraday/day trading — same trading day

### Strategy concepts

- Trend following
- Breakout setups

## Final Milestone

Before moving toward any real-money activity, the learner should be able to explain:

- what is being traded;
- how the order reaches the market;
- why price can move;
- where the trade becomes invalid;
- how much can be lost;
- what the expected reward is;
- and why the trade is being taken.
