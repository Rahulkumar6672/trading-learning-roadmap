# 📖 Trading Terms Glossary

This file will grow throughout the course.

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Trading | Buying and selling an asset with the aim of benefiting from price movement. |
| Stock/Share | A unit representing ownership in a company. |
| Stock Market | A marketplace where shares and other securities can be traded. |
| Broker | A regulated intermediary/platform through which market orders are placed. |
| Exchange | A market infrastructure where eligible securities can be traded. |
| SEBI | India's securities-market regulator. |
| LTP | Last Traded Price — the price of the most recent completed trade. |
| Bid | Price a buyer is currently willing to pay. |
| Ask | Price a seller is currently willing to accept. |
| Spread | Difference between the best ask and best bid. |
| Volume | Number of units/shares traded during a period. |
| Market Cap | Market value of a company's outstanding shares. |
| Candlestick | A chart representation showing open, high, low and close for a period. |
| Support | A price area where buying interest may appear. |
| Resistance | A price area where selling pressure may appear. |
| Stop Loss | A predefined exit level intended to limit a trade's loss. |
| Target | A predefined price level at which a trader may take profit. |
| Risk/Reward | Comparison between potential loss and potential profit. |
| Leverage | Using borrowed/exposed capital to increase position size; it also increases risk. |
| Futures | Derivative contracts whose value is linked to an underlying asset. |
| Options | Derivative contracts giving the holder specified rights under the contract, subject to its terms. |
